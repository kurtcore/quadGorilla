About

Ben Dragstrem

Founder of QuadGorilla Fitness, certified paramedic, and a lifter for more than 15 years.

My background in emergency medicine gives me a close look at how the body responds to stress, load and recovery. I've spent years applying that to my own programming: reading the science, testing it on myself, and keeping what works.

My training is rooted in Westside conjugate principles, with max effort, repetition effort and dynamic effort work, adapted for people with real lives and real goals. Whether you're stepping on a competition stage, passing a PT test, or building a stronger version of yourself, the approach is the same: smart, progressive, sustainable work.

My goal has always been bigger than my own gains. I built QuadGorilla to help build a healthier America, one rep at a time.
