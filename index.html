How coaching works

  1. Tell Ben your goal

    Fill in a two-minute form: your goal, experience, days you can train, and any injuries.

  2. Get your program

    Ben matches you to a program and sends it with your weekly goal tracker, usually within 24 hours.

  3. Check in every week

    Set your goals on Monday, report back on Friday, and get feedback that adjusts the next week.

The method

Programming rooted in Westside conjugate principles, adapted for people with jobs, families and real schedules.

Max effort

Heavy compound lifts worked up to a top set. This is where strength is built and measured.

Repetition effort

Accessory work in the 8 to 15 rep range to build muscle and shore up weak points.

Dynamic effort

Lighter loads moved fast: jumps, sprints, speed work. Power you can use outside the gym.

Your coach

A paramedic's understanding of how the body handles stress, load and recovery, applied to programming that is tested on himself first.
