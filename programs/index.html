Fat Loss

Comp Prep

Military Readiness

Ongoing

For National Guard, active duty, fire and EMS. Keeps you ready for your fitness test and the job: strength, running, carries and work capacity on a schedule that fits drill and shifts.

Starter

Nutrition

Whole foods and a simple structure. No obsessive tracking. Adjust portions to your goal.

Sample day

MealOption AOption B (vegetarian)Rough estimate
Breakfast4 eggs, turkey sausage, whole-grain toastGreek yogurt, berries, oats~500 cal, 35 g protein
LunchGrilled chicken, brown rice, broccoliChickpea salad with quinoa~600 cal, 45 g protein
DinnerSalmon, sweet potato, asparagusTofu stir-fry with brown rice~650 cal, 40 g protein
SnacksProtein shake and almondsHummus, vegetables, crackers~250 cal, 20 g protein
  • Protein: about 0.8–1 g per pound of bodyweight.
  • Fats: olive oil, avocado, nuts and fatty fish daily.
  • Carbs: time most of them around training.
  • Prep on Sunday: cook proteins and grains in bulk to keep the week simple.

These are general targets for healthy adults. If you have a medical condition, food allergy or a history with dieting, work out your plan with your doctor or a registered dietitian.
